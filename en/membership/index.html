Membership & affiliations

The organizations I belong to — in Norway and in the Czech Republic.

Beekeeping is a community. These are the organizations I belong to and work with — both in Norway, where I keep bees today, and in the Czech Republic, where my roots lie.

Member

Norges Birøkterlag

Norway

The Norwegian Beekeepers' Association is the national federation for beekeepers in Norway. As a member, I am part of a nationwide professional community promoting good beekeeping, bee health and knowledge-sharing.

Visit website
Workshop

Bergen Fellesverksted

Bergen, Norway

An open membership workshop in Bergen equipped for woodworking. This is where I build my hives by hand.

Visit website
Member

Boskovice Beekeepers' Association

Boskovice, Czech Republic

The local beekeepers' association in Boskovice, where I am still listed as a member to this day. It was at a meeting here that I first heard doc. Přidal lecture on varroa — the meeting that drew me into academic beekeeping.
