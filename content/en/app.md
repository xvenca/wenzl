# Beekeeping App

## Upcoming App for Beekeepers
Track your bees, make notes, and manage your hives locally. An app designed specifically for Norwegian beekeepers and our conditions.

## Free for Norwegian Beekeepers
The app will be freely available for all Norwegian beekeepers. Coming soon to Google Play and App Store.

## Features
- Tracking of hive activity
- Notes and observations
- Local data management
- Adapted to Norwegian conditions and seasons

*Stay tuned - the app launches soon!*
